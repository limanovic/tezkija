# Privacy Policy — Daily Qur’an

_Last updated: 30 July 2026_

## Summary

Daily Qur’an does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline on your device.

## Data collection

We collect **no data**. Specifically:

- No account or sign-up is required or possible.
- The app makes no network requests. All Qur’an text is bundled with the app in a local database.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
- No crash reports or usage statistics are transmitted.

## Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it:

- Your settings (notification times, language, passage length, theme).
- Your bookmarks.
- A local log of passages already delivered, used to avoid repetition.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

## Notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device at the times you choose. Notifications are generated on-device; no notification service or external server is involved.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — to deliver your daily passages. Requested from you in-app; optional.

No other permissions are requested. The app has no access to your files, contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date. Since the app has no network access, any material change would only come with an app update.
